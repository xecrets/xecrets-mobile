# Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Xecrets Ez mobile applications for Android and iOS,
developed by Svante Seleborg and published by Axantum Software AB.

## No Data Collection

Xecrets Ez does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. The app has no
analytics, advertising, telemetry, user accounts or backend service, and it does not
connect to the Internet on its own.

Files, passwords, encryption keys and app activity remain on the device except when
you explicitly use an operating-system feature that transfers data elsewhere, as
described below.

## Files and Passwords

Xecrets Ez processes files that you select, open or send to the app. Encryption and
decryption take place locally on the device. Passwords are used locally and are not
stored or transmitted by the app.

The app may create temporary copies of files while encrypting, decrypting, previewing,
editing, opening or sharing them. It attempts to overwrite and remove its tracked
temporary files when they are no longer needed. Because mobile operating systems,
flash storage and other applications control parts of the storage lifecycle, secure
physical erasure cannot be guaranteed.

## Profile, Settings and Device Backup

The app stores a local profile containing the key material required for its operation.
The profile key pair is encrypted with the profile password, which is not stored with
it. App settings are stored using the operating system's secure-storage facilities.

The device operating system may include the encrypted profile in its cloud backup or
device-transfer service, according to the user's platform and account settings. Xecrets
Ez does not perform or control that transfer and does not have access to the backup.
Secure app settings are excluded from Android backup, and Apple secure-storage items
are configured for use on the current device only.

## Crash Reports

If the app crashes, it writes a diagnostic report to its local cache. The report may
contain the app version, operating-system version, device manufacturer and model,
runtime and processor architecture, and technical exception details. It is not sent
anywhere automatically.

When the app next displays the crash report, it copies the report to the device
clipboard so that the user can review and paste it into a support request. Clipboard
contents may be accessible to the operating system and, subject to platform controls,
other applications. Opening the support site does not submit the report; the user
decides whether to send it.

The operating system may separately collect crash or diagnostic information under the
user's platform privacy settings. Such collection is controlled by Apple, Google or the
device provider, not by Xecrets Ez.

## File Pickers, Sharing and Other Applications

Xecrets Ez uses system-provided file pickers, document providers, save dialogs, sharing
interfaces and application launchers. When the user chooses a file through one of
these interfaces, the selected storage provider supplies it to the app. When the user
chooses to save, open or share a file, the operating system or selected receiving
application may receive that file, including decrypted content.

Those services and applications are independent of Xecrets Ez and operate under their
own privacy policies. Xecrets Ez does not control what they do with data the user
chooses to provide to them.

## Children

Xecrets Ez does not knowingly collect personal data from children or anyone else.
